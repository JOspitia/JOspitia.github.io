Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-16 · Effective immediately

This page documents what data jospitia.github.io collects about you, and how to opt out. The short version: nothing personally identifiable, ever, by default.

──────────────────────────────────

1. Data I do NOT collect

The site itself does not set any cookies, does not run any analytics scripts by default, and does not embed any third-party trackers. There is no form on the page that POSTs data to any backend.

2. Third-party requests (the only data flow)

When your browser loads a page it makes a small number of standard third-party requests. These are not under my control but I list them here for transparency:

  • Google Fonts (Inter + JetBrains Mono): CSS + font-file requests to fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the static files; logs (IP + user agent) are stored by GitHub per their privacy policy.

3. Optional analytics (Plausible)

If I ever enable privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible.io) it will be added after a notice on this page. Plausible itself is cookie-less, GDPR/CCPA compliant, and only records aggregated counts (page views, referrer, country). No personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking.

4. AI crawlers

The robots.txt explicitly allows AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Applebot-Extended, Meta-ExternalAgent) so they can cite this site when answering questions about the author. They may retain copies of public pages for training or retrieval. This is opt-in from the site owner's side; you can opt out at the browser level by using tools like NoScript or DuckDuckGo.

5. Contact data you send me

If you email me (jjohanospitia@gmail.com), message me on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or any other channel listed on the contact section, the data you share is held by those platforms under their own privacy policies and is not stored anywhere on jospitia.github.io.

6. Your rights

  • Right to know what is collected (this page answers that).
  • Right to deletion: contact me and I'll delete any records I hold.
  • Right to opt out of analytics: do-not-track header is respected by Plausible.

7. Changes to this policy

Any change bumps the "Last updated" date above. Material changes (e.g. enabling analytics) will also be announced on the homepage.
